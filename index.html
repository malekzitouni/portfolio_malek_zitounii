Wolof-Adapted Industry-Ready Open-Weight Language Models

Gwénolé Lecorvé, Delphine Charlet, Pierre Adam, Floriane Behanzin, Lionel Delphin-Poulat, Johannes Heinecke, Géraldine Damnati, Pelin Kaytmaz, Nourredine Khaous, Charbel Kindji, Meriam Maadi, Abdoulaye Mbaye, Claire Perroux, Ismaël Rousseau, Papa Séga Wadé, Gilles Xiberras, Olivier Zhang, Malek Zitouni

What it’s about

Wolof is spoken by about 23 million people, mostly in Senegal, yet open-weight language models support it poorly — and API-only models raise cost, governance and sovereignty concerns for local deployments. The paper presents open-weight models from 0.6B to 14B parameters adapted to Wolof through instruction tuning, preference alignment and model merging. They are being evaluated in internal customer-care pilots and are publicly available.

My contribution

  • Tokenizer adaptation and vocabulary extension for Wolof on Qwen2.5-7B
  • Embedding initialization for new rows: mean, random and CW2V
  • CPT and instruction-tuning pipelines on HPC with DeepSpeed ZeRO-3
  • Evaluation on 35+ Wolof tasks: perplexity, METEOR, accuracy, exact match, char edit distance, LLM-as-judge
